# Product law

Yours is the home and the studio. Other apps stay connected. They are not the landlord.

## Floors

- Home: `/u/you` — page you design, shop, live door, podcast.
- Playground: make, clip, post, vlog. Likes and follows accrue to the person.
- Desk: one start for live, breath on the pane that gifted, calendar, track board.

## Not Twitch

We do not replace their discover page. We save the hours of four dashboards and keep the thank-you on the house.

## Official only

OAuth + PKCE. No passwords for satellites. No scrape. Edit the master on Yours; push copies where APIs allow.
